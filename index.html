Questions

Straight answers.

What to bring: your title (or loan info), photo ID, registration, and every key and fob.

Do I have to sell if I check my range?

No. The range is free and you'll only hear from us by email unless you ask for a firm offer.

Do I have to buy a car from you?

No. You can sell us your car and walk out with a check. If you want your next car from us, your offer goes toward it.

How long is my offer good for?

Seven days, as long as the condition and miles match what you told us.

What if the inspection finds something?

Your buyer shows you what they found and what it changes. Accept the updated number or walk away, no charge.

I still owe money on my car. Can I sell it?

Yes. We get the payoff from your lender and pay it directly.

How do I get paid?

By check, the same day the inspection and paperwork are done.
